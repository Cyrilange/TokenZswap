## Key Concepts

- **ERC-20** → Fungible tokens
- **ERC-721** → Non-fungible tokens (NFTs)
- **approve()** → Authorizes another address or contract to spend or transfer tokens on your behalf
- **transferFrom()** → Allows an authorized address or contract to transfer tokens on your behalf
- mak**Escrow** → The smart contract temporarily holds an asset until the transaction is completed or cancelled
- **LP Token** → Represents a user's share of a liquidity pool
- **AMM (Automated Market Maker)** → Enables token swaps using a mathematical pricing formula instead of a traditional order book
